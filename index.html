How to travel

Short central trip
Walk first. London is often easier to understand on foot.
Normal city trip
Use Tube/Elizabeth line/DLR depending on destination. Pay contactless or Oyster.
Scenic trip
Consider a bus or Thames river service when the route itself is part of the experience.
Airport / outer London
Check the exact fare and route in TfL's journey planner before leaving.
